Press Kit

Pitch

Into The Haunted Land is a multiplayer co-op survival horror game where players enter a forbidden haunted island, search abandoned rooms for valuable cursed items, and try to escape with enough profit to meet the team's quota.

Every expedition asks the group to balance greed, time, light, hiding spots, and the route back to the car. The longer the team stays, the more dangerous the ruins become. If the quota is missed, a last-chance roulette ritual decides whether the run can continue.

Description

Plan each expedition with your team, manage limited inventory space, and return to the car before the haunted land turns against you.
