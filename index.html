Press Kit

Recover cursed relics. Get paid. Leave before the land wakes up.

Into The Haunted Land is a multiplayer co-op survival horror game where players enter a forbidden haunted island, search abandoned rooms for valuable cursed items, and try to escape with enough profit to meet the team's quota.

Every expedition asks the group to balance greed, time, light, hiding spots, and the route back to the car. The longer the team stays, the more dangerous the ruins become. If the quota is missed, a last-chance roulette ritual decides whether the run can continue.

Core Loop

Explore, extract, pay the quota

  • Enter haunted locations with friends and navigate connected rooms, locked doors, hiding spots, and supernatural events.
  • Find regular and cursed items, manage a small backpack, and decide what is worth carrying back to the car.
  • Use light, stamina, voice, and room knowledge to survive enemies that patrol, chase, and search hiding places.
  • Sell recovered items to progress through day-based quota rounds, then face a final-chance roulette if the team falls short.
