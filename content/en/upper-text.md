##### Enter the Haunted Land with friends. Loot with joy. Escape in TERROR.

[Into The Haunted Land](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1917470) is a 1–6 player online co-op horror extraction game. Team up with friends to explore haunted ruins, loot cursed items, hide from monsters, and escape alive. Work together to survive the dangers of the Haunted Land while trying to meet the Guild’s quota.
