Developed by [Kevin Lai](https://x.com/LaiLie0126), one of the developers of [Bionic Bay](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1928690/Bionic_Bay/), this time players will team up and enter a cursed island and explore haunted ruins.

### Core Loop: Explore! Loot! Hide! Extract!

Into The Haunted Land is a 1-6 player multiplayer horror game where players enter haunted ruins, search for valuables, and try to escape alive. The ruins are filled with monsters and strange phenomena, so players must run, hide, and make quick decisions to survive.

A greedy guild demands that your team turn in a quota of valuable items before time runs out, forcing players into one brutal expedition after another. But the item inside the ruins are cursed. Picking one up might shrink you, take away your voice, turn you into a spider, trap you inside a house, or trigger other dangerous and ridiculous effects!

### Cursed Items, Monsters, and Hiding

##### Cursed Items

Cursed items have many different effects. They can be the key to meeting your quota, but also they can turn a successful expedition into a disaster. The team must decide who should carry each item, how dangerous it is, and whether it is time to retreat.

Each ruin also contains a special Omen Item. Taking it will make the game far more difficult and terrifying, but collecting Omen Items is the only way to progress through the main story.

##### Monsters

Each monster has its own behavior, personality, and attack patterns. Some will ignore players until angered. Others may attack the team from the shadows, curse unlucky explorers, or simply attack anyone they see.

Learning how each monster behaves is part of the game, and in the chaos of co-op, one small mistake can lead to trouble, panic, and hilarious moments.

##### Hiding Spots

Players can hide in many places, such as under beds, inside cabinets, and even behind curtains. Not all hiding spots are equally safe. You can hide before being discovered, or dive into cover while being chased and hope the monster did not see you.

Monsters and cursed items can also affect your chances of being found while hiding. A walkie-talkie going off at the wrong time, or a careless friend opening the door you are hiding behind, can quickly become part of the fun.

### Time, Events, and Weather

The ruins change over time. Players arrive during the day, but the world gradually grows darker as the expedition continues, and monsters begin to gather. Make sure you leave before midnight, or the driver and your teammates(if any remain!) will abandon you.

Each expedition can also trigger unpredictable events, such as objects suddenly falling, looping spaces, sudden team separation, and other supernatural phenomena. After taking an Omen Item, special events and special mechanics may also appear.

Weather is another important system. Each day can begin with different conditions, such as rain or dense fog, and the ruins will change depending on the weather.

### Perspective and Visual Style

Unlike many first-person co-op horror games, Into The Haunted Land uses a top-down perspective, giving players a wider view of the environment, their teammates, and nearby dangers. This creates a different kind of horror: not just jump scares, but the tension of watching monsters approach, seeing teammates panic, and feeling cursed effects slowly spiral out of control.

This perspective also makes the game more approachable for players who struggle with motion sickness in 3D games, allowing people who usually have to watch their friends play first-person horror games to join the expedition themselves.
